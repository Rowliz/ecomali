Our Story

Eco-Mali Exchange Limited was founded by Wilberforce Tari and co-founded Collins Omondi in 2025 to connect waste generators with licensed recyclers and community partners, improving collection, diversion and circular value chains.

What started as a community cleanup initiative quickly evolved into a tech-driven solution when we realized the potential of connecting waste generators with recyclers through digital means.

Today, we are a registered youth-led enterprise working with county governments, NGOs, and private sector partners to create a circular economy that benefits everyone.
