Report a Dump Site

Help us keep our environment clean by reporting dump sites and waste disposal areas.

Why Report?

Dump sites pose serious threats to:

  • Public health and safety
  • Environmental quality
  • Community well-being
  • Water and soil contamination

What to Include

  • Exact location or nearest landmark
  • Type of waste dumped
  • Estimated size of dump site
  • Photos if possible
  • Any environmental concerns

Your Privacy

Your report is anonymous. We only use your contact information to follow up if needed.

You can upload multiple images (max 5MB each)
