Full compliance with National Environment Management Authority (NEMA) waste management regulations, ensuring all waste handling and disposal activities meet national environmental standards.

ISO 14001 – Environmental Management Systems

Certified for environmental management systems, demonstrating our commitment to minimizing environmental impact and continuous improvement in environmental performance.

ISO 9001 – Quality Management Systems

Certified quality management system ensuring consistent delivery of high-quality services, customer satisfaction, and continuous improvement processes.

ISO 45001 – Occupational Health & Safety
