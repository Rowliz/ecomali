Why Partner With Eco-Mali Exchange?

Access to Waste Data

Get comprehensive insights from our waste mapping and digitization efforts. Access real-time data on waste streams, collection patterns, and recycling rates.

Youth Network

Connect with our network of 300+ environmentally active youth in Kirinyaga. Tap into a motivated, tech-savvy workforce.

Community Reach

Access 15+ communities across Kirinyaga through our established networks. We have deep roots in the communities we serve.

Traceable Value Chain

Transparent and digitized recycling value chain for accountability. Track every transaction from waste generator to recycler.

Measurable Impact

Clear metrics and reporting on environmental and social impact. See the real difference your partnership makes.

Innovation Platform

Co-create solutions using our digital waste exchange platform. Collaborate on innovative waste management solutions.
