3.2 Commission Structure

EcoMali charges a commission on completed transactions based on total transaction value:

  • 5% commission for transactions ≥ KES 100,000
  • 6% commission for transactions ≥ KES 50,000
  • 7% commission for transactions ≥ KES 10,000

Commission is automatically calculated and deducted from the transaction amount before payment to the waste generator.
